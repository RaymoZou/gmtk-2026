# GMTK 2026 - COUNTDOWN

## THEME: 9-5 Warrior
You are counting down the hours at your typical 9-5 job but before you leave you need to finish your tasks.
You work a 9-5 job and must complete mundane office tasks (photocopying papers, sending emails, etc.) before you can leave by the end of the day.

TASK
- Failing to complete a task will result in 2 more tasks being added
- Some tasks are time-sensitive and must be before, during or after a certain time

- example: Photocopy the papers before 10am
- example: Email the reports BEFORE 12pm today
- example: Call a vendor to fix the leaky pipe
- example: Call the boss BEFORE 5pm
- example: Refill the coffee machine BEFORE 5pm
- example: Find the folder and put it in the file cabinet

DAYS
- Each day has a starting number of tasks that need to completed by the end of the day. 
- Each day has a condition that must be met otherwise it's Game Over
    - example: "Complete 2 tasks"
    - example: "Complete Task #1"
- If you finish all your tasks before the end of the day you can sit at your desk and wait

STAMINA
- You start the day with 100 stamina
- You lose 1 stamina every 2 seconds
- Eating food will +10 stamina
- Drinking coffee will increase temporarily increase your efficiency for tasks by 1.5x for 60 seconds.
- Making eye contact with a coworker will cause an unskippable cutscene and -10 stamina
- If your stamina reaches 0. It will affect your stamina for the next day up to maximum deficit of -30.
